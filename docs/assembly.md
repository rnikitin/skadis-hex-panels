# Assembly and printing

## First test

Open the current [Bambu Studio test project](../models/current/projects/corner_fit_test_24x8x3p2_P2S.3mf). It contains three corner fragments and four identical keys: two for the assembly, two spares. The two existing fragments represent neighbours on a horizontal and an oblique seam.

Print with the flat faces on the bed. The supplied P2S profile uses a 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, PLA, four walls, 100% infill, and Arachne walls. The current project estimate is approximately 1 h 48 min / 65.51 g. Printing has not been performed by this project workflow.

For a tabletop wall simulation, support the existing fragments face-up with at least 8 mm of free space under their rear ribs. First practise access and alignment without adhesive. Insert a key into each existing fragment from its free edge, then move the new fragment toward the simulated wall so both rear-open pockets engage.

Check that the keys stay in place while handling, both directions engage together, the faces settle correctly, the key can release on removal, and an accessory hook can move downward beside the shortened perimeter rib. Stop forcing a tight fit and adjust the key/pocket dimensions after measuring the print.

## Wall assembly

1. Insert a metal M4 nut into each shoe through its side channel. A small piece of thin tape over the channel retains the loose nut; it does not carry the clamping load.
2. Attach four shoes to the panel using M4 screws and flat washers. Upper shoes have more adhesive area above the screw; rotate the lower shoes 180°.
3. Apply a 30 × 50 mm adhesive strip to each shoe. Practise the sequence before exposing the adhesive.
4. Pre-position the hidden keys in the free edges of the already installed neighbours.
5. Bring the new panel toward the wall, engage its rear-open pockets, set the nominal 0.3 mm seam, and secure its own shoes.

The pockets include installation clearance; they do not replace checking the slot alignment and seam while positioning the adhesive mounts.

## Removal

Release the panel's four wall screws and move the panel away from the wall. The spring tips are intended to pass the small retaining ramps in reverse. The removal force and cycle life have not yet been physically tested.

The wall shoes remain independently attached. Their approximately 4 mm front screw-head/washer projection may interfere with a wide accessory spanning a mounting point; verify the intended accessories.
