The user selected the **5.2 mm** PETG width strip: it fit more closely than the smaller candidates. No measured insertion force or printed dimensions were supplied.

The next full-size kit keeps the 5.2 × 15.2 mm capsule section and increases locator depth from 3.5 to 4.4 mm. A pull knob was added at the user's request, then removed because it required excessive supports. The current jig has a flat 4 mm body and prints without supports.

The user selected the smallest proposed wall-shoe pilot, **2.2 mm**, for the purchased nominal 3 × 16 mm self-tappers and requested no further separate calibration print. Full-panel printing and assembly are the next physical test.

Panels will be printed in both PLA and PETG; shoes and jig use PETG. The accepted strip fit is evidence for the PETG width choice, not proof of the deeper jig fit on both panel materials or screw retention.
