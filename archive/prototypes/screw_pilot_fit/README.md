# Nominal 3 × 16 mm self-tapping wall-mount trial

Start with `projects/self_tapping_3x16_pilot_test_P2S.3mf`: about **40 minutes / 21.42 g PLA**. It contains a three-post pilot block (2.2 / 2.4 / 2.6 mm, large engraved labels) and the separate **5 mm test plate**.

Drive the actual screw through that plate into a fresh pilot. Do not fully drive a bare 16 mm screw into the uncovered 13.5 mm-deep pilot. Begin with 2.4 mm and compare the other candidates as needed, using controlled hand tightening. Check seating, cracking, stripping, retention and repeated removal.

`projects/self_tapping_3x16_fit_P2S.3mf` is the larger complete trial, approximately **1 h 21 min / 47.58 g**. It adds a candidate shoe with a 2.4 mm pilot and a panel fragment with two complete SKÅDIS slots and a 3.4 mm mounting clearance hole.

Three complete shoe STL variants are included for choosing the pilot after testing. The 2.4 mm candidate is provisional; physical fit has not been checked.

The supplier diagram gives a 3 mm major diameter, 16 mm under-head length, and 4.9 mm diameter × 3 mm high head. Through the 5 mm panel, 11 mm enters the shoe including the point. There is 2.5 mm clearance below the tip and 7 mm to the adhesive surface.

This shoe has no metal nut pocket. It is a separate variant from the released M4 nut shoe. The small head is intended for the matching 3.4 mm mounting hole, rather than the released 4.5 mm hole.

See [full fit instructions](../../../docs/self-tapping-3x16.md). `parameters.json` records nominal dimensions and unresolved physical checks; `print_metrics.json` identifies the statistics for each sliced project.
